# Code of Conduct

We want Komodo to be a welcoming project. Everyone taking part — in issues,
pull requests, discussions and any other project space — agrees to:

- be respectful and assume good faith; disagree with ideas, not people;
- accept and give constructive feedback gracefully;
- not harass, demean or discriminate against anyone for who they are;
- not publish others' private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules and may
temporarily or permanently ban people who repeatedly do. To report a problem,
email the maintainer at lukasperks@gmail.com; reports are handled privately.

This is a short statement of intent based on the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
